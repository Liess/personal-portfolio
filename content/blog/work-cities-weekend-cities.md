There are cities you visit because you *want* to be there.

And then there are cities you visit because someone put them on the **project plan.** 😂

My usual work city is **BGC**.

For a lot of people, BGC is a place to spend the weekend—walking around, taking photos, checking out restaurants, and being amazed by all the tall buildings.

For me?

**It's the office.** 😅

After spending the whole day surrounded by skyscrapers, those buildings don't really feel impressive anymore. Sometimes they feel more like **cell bars**, trapping me inside for eight hours. 🏢🔒

That's why after work, I like going somewhere above it all—a rooftop bar, a good drink, and a view of the city. 🍸🌃

But not every city is a work city or a weekend escape.

## The in-between city: Quezon City 🏠

**Quezon City is home.**

It's where I reside, and ironically, my main office is basically my condo room. 😂

I can spend the whole day working there—laptop open, meetings, emails, deadlines, the whole thing.

But once work ends, I don't have to travel somewhere far just to switch off.

I just **go outside.**

Gym. 🏋️

Korean BBQ. 🥩

Bars and drinks. 🍻

Badminton. 🏸

Random late-night food trips.

Whatever I feel like doing that day.

That's what makes QC different.

It's not really an escape from work, because work is literally where I live.

Instead, it's a place where **work and life exist in the same area without necessarily being the same thing.**

I close the laptop, walk outside, and suddenly I'm doing something completely unrelated to work.

## Then there are my weekend cities 🌿

**Antipolo** — for the cold breeze, good restaurants, and overlooking views. 🍽️🌬️

**Tagaytay** — for a quick escape and, obviously, **bulalo.** 🥣

**Makati Poblacion** — for parties, drinks, and questionable decisions. 🍻🎶

**La Union** — for beach parties, surfing, and being close enough to the city but far enough to forget about work. 🏄‍♂️🌊

And my favorite:

**Sagada.** 🏔️

The *real* escape.

Cold weather, mountains, nature, quiet roads—and most importantly, **no signal.** 😂

No emails.

No Teams.

No "I'll just quickly check this."

No choice but to disconnect.

## Three kinds of cities

I guess I have three different versions of a city.

**BGC** is where I work. 💻
**Quezon City** is where I live. 🏠
**Sagada, La Union, Tagaytay, Antipolo, and Poblacion** are where I escape. 🌴

Each one serves a different purpose.

The project plan decides where I work.

Home gives me somewhere to return to.

And the weekend gives me somewhere to disappear.

**Maybe that's my version of work-life balance—not keeping work and life in completely different places, but knowing when to switch between them.** ✈️🌃
